Privacy Policy for NetProbePRO Android

Effective date: 2026-06-03

NetProbePRO is a network diagnostic and IT troubleshooting application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how NetProbePRO accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the app.

1. Information We Access or Collect

Network and device information

The app may access network-related information such as WiFi SSID, local IP address, gateway IP address, subnet information, DNS results, ping results, traceroute results, port scan results, SSL certificate information, speed test results, and network monitor results. This information is used to provide network diagnostic functions.

Location permission

On Android, location permission may be required to access WiFi network information such as SSID and related WiFi details. NetProbePRO does not use this permission for GPS tracking or user location tracking.

User-provided information

Users may enter information such as customer name, site name, engineer name, notes, target IP addresses, hostnames, SSH device information, and report details. This information is used to create diagnostics, reports, site profiles, and saved troubleshooting records.

Purchase information

If you purchase NetProbePRO Pro, purchase processing is handled by Google Play Billing. NetProbePRO may receive purchase status information to unlock paid features.

Advertising data

NetProbePRO uses Google AdMob to show ads to free users. Google AdMob may collect or process device identifiers, advertising ID, app activity, approximate location, and other data according to Google’s advertising and privacy practices.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Run network diagnostics and troubleshooting tools.
  • Generate local diagnostic explanations.
  • Create and save reports.
  • Save site/customer profiles.
  • Enable Pro features and remove ads for paid users.
  • Show ads to free users.
  • Improve app reliability and user experience.

3. Data Storage

Most diagnostic information, reports, notes, and site profiles are stored locally on your device. NetProbePRO does not currently provide cloud account sync. If cloud features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

4. Data Sharing

NetProbePRO does not sell your personal information.

Data may be shared or processed by third-party services used by the app, including Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Billing for purchases and subscription management.

If you choose to share a report using WhatsApp, email, messaging apps, or other sharing tools, the shared report is handled by the app or service you select.

5. SSH and Network Credentials

If you use SSH features, you may enter device login information such as host, username, port, password, or command details. This information is used only to connect to the selected network device.

You should not share reports containing sensitive credentials. NetProbePRO is designed for troubleshooting and should be used responsibly on networks and devices you own or are authorized to test.

6. Permissions

NetProbePRO may request the following Android permissions:

  • Internet access: required for network tests, DNS, speed tests, ads, and purchases.
  • Network state access: required to detect connection status.
  • WiFi state access: required to read WiFi/network details.
  • Location permission: may be required by Android to access WiFi SSID and WiFi details.
  • Notifications or foreground service permissions: may be used for monitoring features.

7. Children’s Privacy

NetProbePRO is intended for IT and network professionals. It is not designed for children.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect locally stored app data. However, no system is completely secure. Users should avoid storing sensitive credentials or confidential customer information unless necessary.

9. User Choices

You can:

  • Delete saved reports and site profiles inside the app.
  • Clear app data from Android settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID through Android/Google settings.
  • Manage purchases and subscriptions through Google Play.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, permissions, or third-party services change. Updated versions will be posted at this privacy policy URL.
